Cornell Alumni in Delhi NCR

Far above Cayuga’s waters, and right here in Delhi.

The Cornell Club of Delhi brings together alumni, students, parents and friends of Cornell University across the National Capital Region.

What we do

Staying connected to Cornell, together

Whether you graduated last spring or decades ago, there’s a place for you here.

Social gatherings

Monthly meetups, festive celebrations and an annual gala for Cornellians and their families.

Professional network

Panels, mentoring and introductions across industries, from policy and finance to tech and hospitality.

Future Cornellians

Send-off parties for admitted students and alumni interviews for applicants from the region.

Our community

Any person, any study, any city

Ezra Cornell’s founding vision was an institution where any person can find instruction in any study. Our club carries that spirit into Delhi, welcoming every Cornellian, whatever their college, class year or career path.
