About the club

Who we are

The Cornell Club of Delhi is a volunteer-led alumni club serving Cornellians who live in or travel through Delhi, Gurugram, Noida and the wider NCR.

We bring people together socially and professionally, support students headed to Ithaca and beyond, and keep our members connected to the university.

Who can join

  • Graduates of any Cornell college or program, including Weill Cornell and Cornell Tech
  • Current students home for the summer or on leave
  • Parents of current and former Cornell students
  • Faculty, staff and friends of the university
